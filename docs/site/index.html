The ToskLight suite

Plan your venue. Build the moment. Run the show.

Draw and print a plan, see the rig before you arrive, program a show that stays understandable, and play video as part of the picture. Use the suite together or connect each application to any Art-Net or sACN-compatible lighting desk.

Open source lighting control desk

ToskLight Control

Easy to enter. Deep enough to grow with you.

Start with direct fixture control and reusable looks. Add tracked cues, Dynamics, command-line programming, and custom workspaces as your show and your confidence grow.

  • Program naturally. Start with direct controls and grow into a desk-style command language.
  • Make changes that hold. Groups and presets keep a look connected to the show, so a correction made in rehearsal follows through the cue list.
  • Connect to the real world. Output through sACN, Art-Net, or USB DMX, with as many universes as your hardware supports. The released desk is tested with eight full universes.

Open source live media server

ToskLight Pixel

Build a complete visual picture from video, images, text, generated visuals, and effects. Run it locally or let any Art-Net or sACN-compatible desk take control.

  • Compose the image live. Stack up to eight independent video layers, shape them, and keep the final composition in view.
  • Use the desk you already know. Patch Pixel into any Art-Net or sACN show, with previews and media choices available when you need them.
  • Make every surface count. Send a deliberate picture to LED walls, projection, off-screen outputs, or a room full of displays.

Open source visualizer and show CAD

ToskLight Architect

Plan a venue in CAD, print the plan, and see the finished rig in a live 3D renderer. Prepare before the desk arrives, then visualize real Art-Net or sACN from ToskLight or another compatible desk.

  • Plan the physical show. Lay out fixtures, scenery, media surfaces, patch, and installed geometry.
  • Move between planning and control. Architect and Control read the same portable .show format.
  • Visualize live. Render beams, focus, color, movement, gobos, lasers, particles, and venue geometry.

Why it exists

Professional concepts should survive a smaller budget.

The industry-standard consoles are excellent and worth every penny. The difficulty sits below that budget: smaller productions often learn a workflow that stops applying the day they decide to take the craft further.

ToskLight bridges that gap with professional concepts, free to use on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Start by pointing at things to get a show running, then grow into the command line, tracking, and referencing as you need them.

A connected production workflow

From first idea to final output.

  1. 01Plan

    Build the venue, rig, patch, and media surfaces in Architect.

  2. 02Program

    Create fixture looks, cues, effects, timelines, and playbacks in Control.

  3. 03Connect

    Patch Pixel outputs as ordinary media fixtures and discover their libraries.

  4. 04Perform

    Run lighting and media from one deliberate operator workflow.
