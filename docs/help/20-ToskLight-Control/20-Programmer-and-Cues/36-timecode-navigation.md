# Timecode navigation

The ruler and linked main audio stay pinned above the scrolling lanes. All lanes share horizontal scrolling and zoom, and the red playhead time remains at the top while you scroll vertically.

## Open lanes and edit views

Each Cuelist has its own compact lane. Tap a Cue to expand its [timing editor](35-timecode-cue-editing.md); **Close cue editor** returns to Cue blocks.

**Speed Groups** appear below the main audio. Speed Group and **Audio Player** sections can fold into one 44-pixel preview row. **Expand** reveals individual lanes. Tap a Speed Group name to enlarge it for editing; a pencil beside the title identifies the edit view.

Selecting a keyframe keeps the lane geometry unchanged and does not edit its time or value. An expanded Speed Group section retains its highlight, with a stronger highlight on the working lane.

## Move markers and the playhead

Marker names and dotted drag grips sit in the ruler and stay visible while lanes scroll vertically. Drag a marker flag to move it. Marker lines are half transparent and do not intercept lane controls; the red playhead stays above them.

Scrub the ruler or drag the playhead to inspect a time. This leaves live output at its current transport position. Press the transport-position button in the title to seek to the editor playhead.

## Use the overview

The overview's outlined window shows the time range visible in the lanes. It uses their time axis, including blank space retained when the window grows or the sidebar closes.

Drag either vertically centered edge grip to change the visible range and zoom. Drag the dotted grip at the top middle to move the range without changing zoom. A grip keeps the point where you grabbed it: pressing without dragging causes no jump or zoom. Pull both edges to the track ends to fit the current viewport.

Use encoder zoom for another way to change the visible range. Opening or closing the selection sidebar preserves the timeline's zoom and left-hand position.
