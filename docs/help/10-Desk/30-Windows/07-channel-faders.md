# Channel Faders

Channels provides a two-row intensity bank, normally 20 faders per page. Fixtures need a physical or virtual dimmer to appear; scenery and other non-level objects remain in Patch and Stage.

Each fader shows the fixture name, Fixture ID, and resolved intensity. Touch the card to select the fixture; move the fader to write its intensity into the desk Programmer. Record or clear those temporary values deliberately.

Use previous/next or the page picker in the full Channels window. A compact pane stays on channels 1–20. These positions are view indices, not DMX addresses.

Disabled faders show their reason, such as an empty position or unavailable Programmer. For other attributes, use All channels mode or the Fixture Sheet. Use DMX to inspect final raw output.
