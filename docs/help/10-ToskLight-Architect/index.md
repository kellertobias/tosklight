# ToskLight Architect

![ToskLight Architect application icon](../assets/branding/tosklight-architect.png)

ToskLight Architect plans the venue and lighting rig before programming begins in Control. Build and patch the rig, position fixtures, prepare drawings, and inspect the result in the Visualizer. The portable `.show` document carries the rig and its embedded fixture profiles.

Start with fixtures, then build the venue around them:

1. [Create and open a rig](01-create-and-open.md): documents, the Demo Show, project information, and multiple windows.
2. [Fixtures and patch](02-Fixtures-and-Patch/index.md): choose fixtures, patch modes and addresses, and edit profiles. [Architect patch views](02-Fixtures-and-Patch/08-architect-patch-views.md) covers sheet editing, layers, and the address grid.
3. [Position, rotate, and align fixtures](03-position-and-align.md): measured placement, spreads, the gizmo, duplication, and snapping.
4. [Build the venue](04-build-the-venue.md): stage decks, trusses, scenery, groups, and imported models.
5. [Draw and print plans](05-draw-and-print-plans.md): views, underlays, annotation, cut planes, and print pages.
6. [Fixture library and MVR](06-library-and-mvr.md): profile revisions and interchange.
7. [Work with Control](07-control-and-transfer.md): publish the rig, keep edits synchronized, and resolve conflicts.
8. [Media surfaces](08-media-surfaces.md): screens, LED walls, projectors, sources, and fallback pictures.
9. [Special effects](09-special-effects.md): lasers, sparks, flames, and released scenery.
10. [Tracking points](10-tracking-points.md): aim points, attached objects, and the distinction from live tracking.
11. [Visualize the rig](11-visualizer.md): preview, live inputs, cameras, and local settings.

[Advanced rig planning](90-Advanced/index.md) covers fixture models, crowd areas, and snapshots and native 4K images.

Architect can stay synchronized with a Control show while cues and playbacks remain under Control's authority. Use [Work with Control](07-control-and-transfer.md) before editing a desk's rig from another computer.
