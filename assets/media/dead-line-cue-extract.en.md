## Cue extract

*English translation of “The Contents of the Package”, pages 51-52 of the author-provided 2018 script.*

Mia has decided that she will accept neither the ID card nor the weapon. Still angry, she walks purposefully to the microwave, throws the ID card inside, and switches it on. In the same motion, she moves toward the weapon and decisively places the box over it.

Trying to distract herself, Mia plugs the stereo back in, opens her laptop, and starts playback. She puts the laptop beside her on the couch. The computer selects a random playlist: Moritz's playlist, with the piano version of **“Mad World.”** Mia restores part of the shrine, finds the photograph from the Semicolon scene, sits down with it, and begins to cry.

## Cues

1. A notification appears on the laptop. The music *fades down* until it remains only in the background; then a notification sound is heard.

   > **MESSAGE - Moritz**<br>
   > Sorry I just left. How did your presentation go?<br>
   > Sent: 7 September 2018 - delivery failed

2. The music rises again, now distorted; **city noise** can be heard.

   **MIA**

   > *(shouts toward the audience)*

3. Mia becomes more desperate. A second notification follows.

   > **MESSAGE - Peter**<br>
   > THANK YOU for supervising my bachelor's thesis five years ago.<br>
   > Without you I would never have ended up here!!!

   **MIA**

   > *(shouts again, quieter than before - more a tearful cry)*

4. Mia makes herself smaller. The city noise increasingly overwhelms the music. A further notification arrives, this time from a social network.

   > **MESSAGE - Social network**<br>
   > Manage estate - Kurt Pellmann named you as his contact for administering his estate. This action is now required.

5. Mia tries to scream, but no sound comes out. She collapses to the floor. The music can no longer be heard; city noise has completely displaced it. The city noise slowly becomes rising **feedback**, growing louder. The world in the window accelerates and disappears. The light darkens and a **spotlight isolates Mia** on the floor.

6. The feedback stops abruptly. Everything is black except the spotlight; the window is black too. Mia sits up mechanically, staring blankly. **[REDACTED]**

## End cue

> **BLACKOUT.**

Mia exits.
