DEAD LINE

Mia has decided that she will accept neither the ID card nor the weapon. Still angry, she walks
purposefully to the microwave, throws the ID card inside, and switches it on. In the same motion,
she moves toward the weapon and decisively places the box over it.

Trying to distract herself, Mia plugs the stereo back in, opens her laptop, and starts playback.
She puts the laptop beside her on the couch. The computer selects a random playlist: Moritz's
playlist, with the piano version of "Mad World". Mia restores part of the shrine, finds the
photograph from the Semicolon scene, sits down with it, and begins to cry.

A notification appears on the laptop. First, the music fades down until it remains only in the
background; then a notification sound is heard. The notification is an SMS from Moritz.

MESSAGE
Sorry I just left. How did your presentation go?
Sent: 7 September 2018 - delivery failed

Shortly afterwards the music rises again, but it now sounds distorted and city noise can be heard.

MIA
(shouts toward the audience)

Mia becomes more desperate. A second notification follows, this time from Peter.

MESSAGE
THANK YOU for supervising my bachelor's thesis five years ago.
Without you I would never have ended up here!!!

MIA
(shouts again, quieter than before - more a tearful cry)

Mia makes herself smaller. The city noise increasingly overwhelms the music. A further
notification arrives, this time from a social network.

MESSAGE
Manage estate - Kurt Pellmann named you as his contact for administering his estate.
This action is now required.

Mia tries to scream, but no sound comes out. She collapses to the floor. The music can no longer
be heard; city noise has completely displaced it. Mia curls up tighter and tighter. The city noise
slowly becomes rising feedback, growing louder. The world in the window accelerates and disappears.
The light darkens and a spotlight isolates Mia on the floor.

The feedback stops abruptly. Everything is black except the spotlight; the window is black too.
Mia sits up mechanically, staring blankly. [REDACTED]

BLACKOUT.

Mia exits.
